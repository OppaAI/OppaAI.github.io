Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What this is

Aidon is a personal AI assistant operated by OppaAI. It communicates via WhatsApp, Slack, and a web interface.

Data collected

Messages you send to Aidon, your phone number / WhatsApp ID, and timestamps. If you ask Aidon to remember something, it stores that in a local database.

How it's used

Solely to generate responses to your messages and carry out tasks you request. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage

All data stays on the operator's own server. WhatsApp messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform as part of message delivery.
